# Editorial Rules v86.9

## Reports
A full results report has priority over ordinary news once the morning hold window has passed. It may bypass generic URL/title/history gates only if it passes the strict `TRUE_RESULTS_REPORT` detector.

## Single news vs report
Post-show single news remains separate from full reports. Debuts, returns, attacks, injuries, and title changes may be published independently, but they must not be treated as full reports.

## Commentary and opinion
Commentary articles are capped unless they contain a concrete new fact. A take from a pundit or former wrestler should not outrank a complete results report.

## CTA and media
Footer questions and comment prompts must be removed, but nearby images and embeds must be preserved if they were extracted as editorial media blocks.
