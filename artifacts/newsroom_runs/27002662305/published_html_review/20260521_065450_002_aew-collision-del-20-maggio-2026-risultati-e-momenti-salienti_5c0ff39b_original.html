AEW Dynamite & Collision Results 5/20 - Two Titles On The Line, Anything Goes Match

By Olivia Quinlan  and Samantha Schipman Updated: May 20, 2026 11:23 pm EST
AEW/Lee South

Welcome to Wrestling Inc.'s live coverage for "AEW Dynamite" and "AEW Collision" on May 20, 2026, coming to you live from the Cross Insurance Arena in Portland, Maine at a special run time of 3 hours!

As he prepares to defend the AEW World Championship against MJF in a Title vs. Hair Match at AEW Double or Nothing this coming Sunday, Darby Allin will be putting his title on the line tonight as he defends against "Speedball" Mike Bailey. Allin has had successful defenses against Tommaso Ciampa, Brody King, Bailey's JetSpeed tag team partner Kevin Knight, PAC, Konosuke Takeshita of The Don Callis Family, and most recently Sammy Guevara across "Dynamite" and "Collision" over the last several weeks dating back to the April 22 episode of "Dynamite".

Two thirds of the AEW World Trios Champions and Conglomeration members Orange Cassidy and Roderick Strong will be challenging FTR for their AEW World Tag Team Championship, with only days remaining before Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler defend their title against Christian Cage and Adam Copeland in an I Quit Match at Double or Nothing. Cassidy defeated Harwood in a Double Jeopardy Match during the "Dynamite" and "Collision" special on May 6 to earn his and Strong's title shot tonight.

Cassidy and Strong's stablemate Mark Briscoe will be competing in a match of his own as he goes head-to-head with Tommaso Ciampa in an Anything Goes Match. Briscoe has been looking to seek out retribution against Ciampa since making his return to AEW, with Ciampa having previously dethroned Briscoe as TNT Champion on the January 31 episode of "Collision" before eventually losing it to Kyle Fletcher shortly after.

Yet another member of The Conglomeration will also be in action, as Kyle O'Reilly faces The Death Riders' Jon Moxley in an AEW Continental Championship Eliminator Match. If O'Reilly emerges as the victor, then he will earn a future shot at Moxley's Continental Championship. Moxley won't be the only person competing in an Eliminator Match, as Divine Dominion's Megan Bayne and Lena Kross have issued an AEW Women's World Tag Team Championship Five Minute Eliminator match to any women's tag team who wishes to answer the call.

Jericho, The Hurt Syndicate's Bobby Lashley and Shelton Benjamin, and The Elite's Kenny Omega, Jack Perry, Matt Jackson, and Nick Jackson will be colliding with The Don Callis Family's Andrade El Idolo and Mark Davis, The Dogs' David Finlay and Clark Connors, and The Demand's Ricochet, Toa Liona, and Bishop Kaun in a Stadium Stampede Match at Sunday's pay-per-view event. Before all fourteen men meet, Jericho will be joining forces with The Young Bucks to take on Andrade, Davis, and Ricochet in trios competition tonight.

Katsuyori Shibata of The Opps will be competing in his first match since taking part in a 21 Man Blackjack Battle Royal for the National Championship during the AEW Revolution Zero Hour Pre-Show on March 15 as he squares off against Will Ospreay. Ospreay has been training with the aforementioned Moxley and the rest of The Death Riders over the last few weeks to better his previously injured neck, and emerged victorious against The Bang Bang Gang's Ace Austin during last Wednesday's episode of "Dynamite".

Additionally, The Brawling Birds' Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor will be joining forces with Thunder Rosa and Mina Shirakawa to face Ring of Honor Women's World Champion Athena and Triangle of Madness' Skye Blue, Julia Hart, and AEW Women's World Champion Thekla in an Eight Woman Tag Team Match. Swerve Strickland will also be making an appearance on tonight's show as he prepares to take on Bandido in a quarterfinals match for the 2026 Men's Owen Hart Foundation Cup Tournament at Double or Nothing.

We are live! Excalibur, Tony Schiavone, and Taz greet audiences at home before we head over to a video of Jericho and The Young Bucks strategizing ahead of their Trios Match against The Don Callis Family and Ricochet earlier today. Jericho also apologizes to Matt Jackson and Nick Jackson about his past actions against them and the three men brainstorming names for their trio. The three men then make their way to the ring, with The Don Callis Family and The Ricochet following.

The Young Bucks and Jericho vs. The Don Callis Family (w/ Don Callis) and Ricochet

AEW/Lee South

Jericho and Ricochet look to begin the action. The bell rings and Ricochet tags out to Andrade El Idolo. Jericho and Andrade lock up, but Jericho runs over Andrade with a shoulder tackle and follows it up with a series of chops on his chest.

Andrade slides out of the ring to take a selfie with a pair of female fans in the crowd, but Jericho is waiting with a chop to level him on the floor and dumps him back in the ring. Nick Jackson tags in and triple teams on Andrade with Matt Jackson and Jerich. Mark Davis then tags in and fires off stomps on Nick in the corner. He lands a bodyslam and a running senton on him, but Matt and Ricochet tag in. Matt lands a dropkick on Ricochet and uses him as a launching pad to deliver a Sliced Bread to Andrade, then sends him crashing into the mat face first.

Back from the break, Jericho and Andrade tag in. Jericho delivers a Bull Dog to Andrade, then knocks Ricochet off the ring apron. Davis slides into the ring, Matt, Nick, and Jericho land triple hurricanranas on Andrade, Davis, and Ricochet before Nick tags back in and tags back out to Matt.

Matt and Nick land a double superkick on Andrade and another one on Davis, then catch Ricochet with a double superkick as Ricochet looks to fly. Andrade sends Nick crashing into the corner face first and delivers The Three Amigos to Matt, then suplexes Nick on top of Matt and sets up for his signature moonsault on him. Matt gets his knees up, and The Young Bucks connect with a BTE Trigger on Andrade. Matt then pins Andrade, but Ricochet lands the Ricosault to break the fall.

Jericho clotheslines Ricochet out of the ring and delivers a double dropkick to him on the outside under the bottom rope, then lands a back suplex on Ricochet off one of the barricades and through two tables in the timekeeper's area.

Matt and Nick land a double superkick on Andrade and follow it up with a BTE Trigger. Matt pins Andrade, but Andrade kicks out. David Finlay and Clark Connors run out to the ring as the referee is distracted by Davis holding a chair, with Connors pulling Nick off the ring apron and Finlay levelling Matt with a kick to his head. Andrade falls on top of Matt to pin him for the win.

Winners: The Don Callis Family and Ricochet

After the match, Toa Liona throws Matt into the corner of the ring and rains down right hands and stomps on him. Jack Perry comes to Matt's aid with a bag of onions in hand that he uses to hit Liona with, but The Hurt Syndicate comes to Perry's aid. The Hurt Syndicate brawls with Davis and Liona through the crowd, and everyone continues to brawl with one another in the ringside area and the crowd.

Tommaso Ciampa then makes his way to the ring. Mark Briscoe follows with a garbage can full of weapons in hand.

Mark Briscoe vs. Tommaso Ciampa in an Anything Goes Match

AEW/Ricky Havlik

The bell rings and Ciampa reaches into the garbage can that Briscoe brought to ringside. He accidentally gets one of his hands caught in a rat trap, and Briscoe takes advantage by firing off right hands on Ciampa. He cracks a mop across Ciampa's spine and sets up a table on the outside, then sits Ciampa in a chair and lands a blockbuster on Ciampa off the ring apron.

Briscoe wraps a chain around Ciampa's neck as Ciampa lays on the table. He ascends to the top rope, but Ciampa escapes and uses the chain to pull Briscoe off the top rope and through the table. Ciampa then gets his hands on a grater and jams it into Briscoe's forehead inside the ring.

Back from the break, Briscoe sprays a fire extinguisher into Ciampa's face. Ciampa hits Briscoe with a baking tray, but Briscoe stays standing and delivers a suplex to Ciampa. Briscoe gets up barbed wire and a staple gun on top of a table on the outside, then hits Ciampa with a garbage can and staples the barbed wire onto the table to hold it in place.

Ciampa cracks the garbage can across Briscoe's spine and jams a part of the barbed wire into Briscoe's head and staples the rest of the barbed wire to the table. Ciampa then staples papers on the commentary desk to Briscoe's head and dumps him inside the ring, then slides a pad full of thumb tacks onto his knee and charges at Briscoe.

Briscoe lands a drop toe hold to send Ciampa crashing into a ladder in the ring, then jams a screwdriver into Ciampa's head. CIampa suplexes Briscoe into the ladder in the corner of the ring and pins him, but Briscoe kicks out and Ciampa catches him with a jumping knee strike using the pad full of thumb tacks. He sets up two chairs facing one another, but Briscoe lands an open palm strike on Ciampa and sits him on the top rope. He places the two chairs back to back and sends Ciampa crashing off the top rope and onto the two chairs.

Briscoe drags Ciampa onto the ring apron and delivers a J-Driller to Ciampa to send him crashing through the table with barbed wire on it. He follows it up with a Froggy Bow to him in the ring for the win.

Winner: Mark Briscoe

We Hear From Swerve Strickland and Prince Nana

AEW/Ricky Havlik

Tony Schiavone looks to introduce Swerve Strickland, but Prince Nana cuts him off and says Schiavone is standing in the ring looking like a substitute teacher trying to control a bunch of fools. He dismisses Schiavone from the ring and gives a grand introduction to Strickland, who then makes his way to the ring.

Bandido attacks Strickland on the entrance ramp and sends him crashing into opposite barricades. Strickland dumps Bandido into the ring, but Bandido rocks Strickland with a right hand and flies over the top rope to level Strickland. He sends Strickland crashing into the ring steps midsection first, but Nana grabs a chair and looks to hit Bandido with it. Bandido sees Nana coming, but Strickland blindsides Bandido from behind and sets up a chair in the ring. Bandido gets Strickland up in a Gorilla Press position and sends him crashing into the mat, then grabs the chair. Strickland grabs the chair once again, but Bandido knocks it out of his hand. Strickland retreats before Bandido can hit him with the chair.

We then head backstage, where Willow Nightingale revealed that she injured her shoulder in her recent match against Red Velvet on this past Saturday's episode of "AEW Collision". She reveals that she has to withdraw from the 2026 Women's Owen Hart Foundation Cup Tournament and relinquish the TBS Championship. 

Back at ringside, Jon Moxley makes his way out. Kyle O'Reilly follows. 

Jon Moxley (w/ Marina Shafir) (c) vs. Kyle O'Reilly in an AEW Continental Championship Eliminator Match

AEW/Lee South

The bell rings and the two lock up. Moxley fires off a chop on O'Reilly in the corner, and the two men exchange right hands and forearms with one another before Moxley levels O'Reilly. O'Reilly grabs his ribs, but Moxley jams his elbow into O'Reilly's head and fires off right hands and left hands on him. He then levels O'Reilly with a big boot.

Back from the break, O'Reilly charges at Moxley off the ring apron. The referee begins a ten count, but both men return to the ring and O'Reilly ascends to the top rope. Moxley trips up O'Reilly and joins him on the ropes, then bites him and rakes his spine with his fingernails. Moxley lands a superplex on O'Reilly as the ring announcer declares that there are only 5 remaining minutes in this match.

O'Reilly cinches in an armbreaker on Moxley, then transitions into an Ankle Lock. Moxley cinches in a Bulldog Choke on O'Reilly, but O'Reilly delivers a dropkick to Moxley as Moxley sits on a chair in the ringside area. He dumps Moxley back inside the ring, but Moxley catches O'Reilly with a cutter and goes for a pin. O'Reilly kicks out.

O'Reilly cinches in a Triangle on Moxley, but Moxley powers up to his feet and exchanges forearms with O'Reilly. O'Reilly cinches in an Angle Lock on Moxley and takes it to the mat, but the time limit expires before Moxley can tap out.

Winner: N/A (time limit draw)

The ring announcer declares that Kyle O'Reilly has earned a Continental Championship shot because he took Moxley to a time limit, but O'Reilly grabs the microphone and says he wants their Continental Championship match to be no time limit.

Athena and Triangle of Madness then make their way to the ring. The Brawling Birds, Thunder Rosa, and Mina Shirakawa follow. 

The Brawling Birds, Thunder Rosa, and Mina Shirakawa vs. Triangle of Madness and Athena in an Eight Woman Tag Team Match

AEW/Ryan Loco

Rosa and Athena begin the action. The bell rings and Rosa levels Atehna with an arm drag. Rosa delivers a springboard crossbody to Athena and pins her, but Athena kicks out and tags in Alex Windsor. Athena catches Windsor with a chop and tags in Skye Blue. Blue and Windsor exchange right hands with one another, but Jamie Hayter tags herself in and double teams on Blue with Windsor. Hayter pins Blue, but Blue kicks out before Shirakawa and Thekla tag in.

Back from the break, Shirakawa catches Athena with a kick as Athena flies through the ropes. She delivers a DDT to her as Rosa fires off a forearm on Blue in the ring. Thekla tags in, and Blue lands a jumping knee on Rosa. She runs the ropes, but Windsor and Hayter trip her as she does so. Hayter levels Blue with a boot on the outside as Hayter holds her in place.

Thekla grabs her AEW Women's World Championship belt, but the referee catches her and disposes of it. As he does so, Thekla sprays her signature mist into Rosa's face and pins her for the win.

Winners: Triangle of Madness and Athena

"Speedball" Mike Bailey then makes his way to the ring. Darby Allin follow, and MJF takes a seat at the commentary desk.

Darby Allin (c) vs. Speedball Mike Bailey for the AEW World Championship

AEW/Ryan Loco

The bell rings and Bailey delivers a dropkick to Allin. He ascends to the middle rope, but Allin moves out of the way and Bailey sends him crashing into one of the barricades on the outside of the ring. He rains down right hands on Allin, then lands a pair of jumping knees on Allin's midsection and delivers a bodyslam to him on the outside of the ring. He stands up on one of the barricades, but Allin trips Bailey and sends him crashing on top of the barricade with a Scorpion Death Drop.

Back from the break, Bailey lands a spinning kick on Allin in the ring and flies off the middle rope with a pair of flipping knees. Allin moves out of the way and delivers a Coffin Drop to Bailey off the ropes, then goes for a pin but MJF puts Bailey's foot on the bottom rope to break the fall and keep him alive in the match.

Kevin Knight runs out to the ring and gets in MJF's face, but Bailey pins Allin. Allin kicks out, but Bailey rolls up Allin once again. Allin kicks out, but Bailey levels Allin with a spinning back heel kick to his head. He follows it up with a thrust kick to him and sits Allin on the top rope, then sets up for a Flamingo Driver. Bailey counters into a pinfall, but Allin reverses into a fall of his own. Bailey kicks out.

Allin delivers a Scorpion Death Drop to Bailey, but struggles to get back to his feet. He fires off stomps on Bailey and cinches in a Scorpion Death Lock on him, but Knight cheers on Bailey. Bailey taps out.

Winner (and still): Darby Allin

After the match, Knight tells Allin that they're counting on him to beat MJF and make him bald/ After Knight and Speedball leave, MJF sneaks back to the ring to attack Allin. MJF got clippers from under the ring and held it to Allin's forehead. Allin got the clippers and held them to MJF, but MJF escaped up the ramp.

Will Ospreay vs. Katsuyori Shibata

AEW

Ospreay comes to the ring first. Shibata follows.

Shibata immediately took him to the mat with an armbar when the bell rang. They exchanged armbars until Ospreay got to the ropes. Ospreay twists Shibata's ankle and rolls Shibata up. Shibata suplexed Ospreay on the apron and again on the floor. Shibata slammed him into the apron and placed Ospreay in a chair before faking him out with a running dropkick before stopping to kick him in the face, sending Ospreay backward.

Back in the ring, Shibata punched him in the corner. Ospreay followed him and landed a dropkick and punches. Shibata punches him in the opposite corner. Ospreay caught him as Shibata was charging at him and kicked him in the face. Shibata rolled to the outside and Ospreay landed a crossbody.

After the break, they exchanged blows before Ospreay took Shibata down. Ospreay followed with an atomic drop. Ospreay landed a flying lariat and then worked his arm. He chopped and kicked him. Shibata with a standing guillotine and backed him into the corner to landed a dropkick. He followed with a half-and-half and a cover. Shibata kicked him in the spine, pulled him up by the hair and kicked him in the spine. Ospreay kicked him in the spine. Shibata got up and Ospreay hit him with an elbow strike and Falcon Arrow. Ospreay went up top and landed an elbow to the back of the head.

Bowens came out and distracted Ospreay, so Shibata connected with a hidden blow. Marina and Claudio come out to back Bowens up the ramp. Shibata low blowed Ospreay and put him in a cross armbreaker. Ospreay stacked him up and powerbombed him before landing Styles Clash. He followed with a Hidden Blade and a high stack roll up for the win.

Winner: Will Ospreay

After the match, Samoa Joe comes out with a mic. "Will and his friends coming to the rescue again." He's here now because of his friend, Shibata. "You chose not to be my friend and you will pay the consequences." He said he'll give him his dream match and will put him to sleep.

Ospreay said this is a dream come true. His nightmare situation is that people are doubting him post injury. 10 years ago, this surgery would've ended his career. He asked Joe how he's going to kill a guy who's already died in the ring. This year the stakes for the Owen are high. The winner gets to wrestle in Wembley. "It's not just a company on my back, it's a whole country, bruv." Death Riders killed the old him and made him an assassin.

RUSH vs. TJ Crawford

AEW

Crawford offered his hand and RUSH took it and didn't let go so he could forearm to the mat. He forearmed him in the corner and double stomped his chest. He landed the horns, pulled him to the middle of the ring and pinned him.

Divine Dominion vs. Kayla Lopez & Elle Valentine - 5 Minute Challenge

AEW

Divine Dominion comes to the ring where their opponents are already waiting.

Bayne sent her to the corner and shoulder tackled her repeatedly. Valentine hit her in the back. Bayne sent her face first into the turnbuckle before hitting her with a lariat. Kross tagged in and hit her repeatedly around the head. She splashed her and hit her with back elbows before doing a tilt-o-whirl slam. She threw her into the corner and Lopez tagged in. Kross took her down with a knee. Bayne and Kross teed off on her. Bayne with a suplex. Kross tagged in and they did stereo delayed vertical suplexes. Bayne picked up Lopez and they double choke slammed her for the win in two minutes and 51 seconds.

Winner: Divine Dominion

FTR vs. Orange Cassidy & Roderick Strong - Tag Team Championship

AEW

Strong and Cassidy come to the ring first. FTR follows, accompanied by Stoke.

FTR attacked them before the bell rang with their jackets still on. Strong knocked Wheeler off the turnbuckle and Cassidy rolled up Dax. FTR got out of the ring and took off their jackets. Strong attacked both members of FTR on the ramp before Cassidy took FTR out with a flying elbow. Strong dumped Dax over his head. He put him on his shoulders and tagged in Cassidy. Cassidy removed his elbow pad and went for an Orange Punch, but Wheeler pulled Dax from the ring before he could connect with it.

After the break, Dax has Strong in a half Nelson. They exchanged strikes before Strong landed a backbreaker. Cash knocked Cassidy off the apron. Strong chopped him. They take each other down with stereo lariats. Cassidy sent Dax face first into the turnbuckle and he followed with a crossbody. FTR picked up Cassidy, but Strong pulled him down. Strong shoved Cash into Dax. Cassidy went for a Sunset Flip and Strong chopped him. Dax kicked out. Cassidy landed a suicida on Dax. Cash tagged in and Dax leap frogged Cassidy and then landed a spinebuster and Cash went for the cover.

Cash pulled Cassidy up by the hair before elbowing him. He landed multiple blows and sent him into the corner and chopped Cassidy to the mat. Cash put him in a Cobra Clutch. Cassidy flipped Cash over and sent him to the corner, allowing Dax to tag in. Dax slammed Cassidy and missed with two elbow drops. Cash charged at Cassidy on the apron and fell off the apron when Cassidy ducked. Dax pulled Cassidy's boot to stop a tag. Cassidy kicked him into the corner. Cash charged at Strong on the apron and they battled. Dax knocked Strong off the apron. He landed a piledriver on Cassidy for 2.5.

After the break, Strong tagged in, but the ref missed it. Dax pulled Cassidy into the corner for him and Cash to pummel. Cash tagged in. Cash charged at Cassidy, but Cassidy moved. Cassidy leapfrogged Dax to tag in Strong. He took him down with a leg lariat and lariat. He kneed Cash in the back before throwing Dax into him. Strong suplexed him. Cassidy tagged in and Strong used him as a weapon on FTR multiple times. Cassidy landed a DDT on Cash and Strong did a baseball slide into Dax on the outside. Cassidy pinned Cash for 2.99. Dax pulled Strong off the apron and sent him into the ringpost. FTR set up for Shatter Machine, but Cassidy got down and rolled up Cash.

Dax kicked Strong into the barricade. Cash tagged in Dax. FTR Orange Cassidy kicked him. Strong kicked Cash out of the ring. Cassidy rolled up Dax for 2.5. Dax chopped Cassidy to the mat. Dax told Stoke to the apron and he pulled powder from his pocket. Cassidy back elbowed Dax and choked Stoke with his jacket. He moved just in time for Dax to punch Stoke. Cassidy rolled Dax up for 2.99. Dax intercepted an Orange Punch, but Cassidy reversed into a Stundog Millionaire. Cassidy was charging in for an Orange Punch, but was intercepted into Shatter Machine. Dax pinned him for 2.99 until Strong broke it up. Strong sends Cash over the ropes, but falls with him. Dax and Cassidy are down in the ring. Dax landed multiple uppercuts and Cassidy rolled him. Dax sent Cassidy towards the ropes and Stoke hit Cassidy in the face with a watch. Dax rolled Cassidy up for the win.

Winner (and still): FTR
