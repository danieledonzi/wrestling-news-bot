# Isolated factual cards and complete pair accounting

Owner authorization: 2026-10-18. Base: main and production `871e54875ea93497c59373199a1320788abcdf2c`.

Raw complete-body anchor/target admission is replaced by source-isolated fact extraction and source-isolated identity/tag enrichment. These are the two operations used in the controlled tests, cached independently against exact source material, title, model and contracts. Statements and source ownership are fixed before enrichment. Complete cards remain directly inspectable in the bounded cache. No primary classification is requested by this path.

History retrieval uses explicit canonical identities and episode facets. Uncertain identity/episode coverage stays eligible for review. Every same-run pair and retained history pair receives ADMIT, NO_SUSPICION or explicit unresolved HOLD. History exclusions carry their own reason and source-card hashes. Batches contain at most 12 pairs and 80,000 prompt bytes; an oversized individual pair holds technically instead of being truncated. Individually valid assessments are cached per pair and survive invalid siblings. Canonical JSON prevents disk key ordering from defeating failed-request backoff. A changed RSS excerpt alone cannot reopen unchanged complete material.

Primary classes, terminal SKIPs, 12-hour successful-publication history, odds exclusions, report/news autonomy, same-run A+B requirements, final two-full-body model/policy/schema and semantic authority remain unchanged. The final verdict cache retains the previous exact-material contract fingerprint; an admission transport change does not reopen a final judgment. Legacy admission readers remain for archived artifacts/tests, but the runtime `_admit` entry point always uses the new path.

## Accounting and noon report

Five canonical event types reconcile card/admission cycles, every eligible pair and captured input inventory. A history exclusion, admission NO_SUSPICION, final NO_MATCH and technical HOLD remain distinct. Sources before capture are not invented into the inventory denominator. The report exposes complete/partial/unavailable coverage, observed versus whole-window totals, missing cycles, cache observations, exclusions and unresolved endpoints. Extraction/enrichment have separate measured economic workloads. Softpool legacy aggregate call counts have explicit card/admission/final-judge breakdowns.

## Material operational limits

Cold material requires two isolated provider calls before reuse. New provenance/uncertainty handling retains **61 of 116** possible history comparisons in the six replay boards, versus 15 in the narrower experimental gate. The extra 46 pairs were not given new live model judgments in this offline replay; missing classification of an episode is deliberately not treated as certain distinction. Do not advertise the prototype's 87% pair reduction as a production saving.

An unavailable current card holds that URL. A completely unavailable published-history card leaves its potential comparisons unresolved and can therefore hold multiple current endpoints until source/card recovery. This is visible, recoverable and never terminal SKIP. Unknown history must not silently certify unrelatedness. First-run card generation and repeated HOLDs must be checked after activation, especially for live strong news. Valid full-body verdicts are not repaired to satisfy local heuristics.

Regal/Samoa Joe thematic admissions, Roman's distinct developments and dedicated-versus-roundup boundaries remain semantic watch cases. Complete accounting is not semantic perfection. No soft-selection reform, model change, report-source change or increase to a 24-hour lookback is included.
