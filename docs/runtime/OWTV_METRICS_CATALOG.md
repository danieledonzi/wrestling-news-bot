# OWTV canonical metrics catalog — v95.19.0

## Contract

The canonical contract is measurement-only. Its policy version is `v95.19.0`; the snapshot schema is `v95.19_observability_snapshot.v3`. Unless stated otherwise, the window is the inclusive UTC interval `[window_start, window_end]`, zero means an available authoritative stream contained no qualifying values, and `null` means the source cannot support the measurement. Canonical content metrics have bounded cardinality (one scalar per snapshot); diagnostic maps have source-dependent cardinality. Daily Editorial Judgment may consume every active metric below. Operational and future Simone reports may consume them only when they preserve the same meanings.

## Diagnostic, deprecated, and planned families

`funnel.event_counts`, `alfred.events.warning_count`, duplicate footprint/fingerprint signals, `simone.legacy_errors_diagnostic`, and Gemini `called_total` / `called_35_total` are diagnostic compatibility fields. `completed_successful_calls` and `gemini_3_5_completed_successful_calls` are deprecated aliases; `called` only means the API returned without an attempt exception. `gemini_3_5_called_total` in Daily Editorial Judgment is deprecated: it is retained for older snapshot/email consumers and, when present, is rendered separately as a diagnostic rather than inferred into canonical counters. Simone terminal errors are **planned** and explicitly unavailable (`null`) until a typed lifecycle error taxonomy exists; the generic handoff error count is not terminal. Full Massy/Andrea item outcomes, Bob error taxonomy, complete Simone lifecycle instrumentation, WordPress telemetry, cost completeness, dashboards, new ledgers, and artifact retention are planned rather than active.

## Implementation notes and checkpoints

Scope is limited to additive snapshot/report measurement; newsroom decisions, prompts, routing, thresholds, publishing, retries, schedules, and state semantics are unchanged. Section metadata records availability, coverage, source, schema/policy versions, warnings, and unavailability reasons. Expected dirt is limited to `.bot_exit_code`, `logs/master_log.log`, and `reports/`; all other tracked or untracked changes remain source diagnostics. Producer detail lists remain capped while `actionable_identity_keys` and `warning_occurrences_total` preserve exact canonical measurements; capped legacy samples yield explicit nulls and diagnostic lower bounds. Check after deployment that identity coverage supports the Menzo ratio, warning events and occurrences diverge as expected, Gemini status totals reconcile to the ledger, future rows stay excluded, and Simone legacy errors remain diagnostic. Deferred refinements are the planned families above, not blockers for this contract.
