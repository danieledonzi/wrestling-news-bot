## Semantic evidence and limitations

Two isolated experiments used 42 real calls to the same installed `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`, unchanged schema
and identical endpoint material across baseline and variant. The request-local references were reconstructed;
these were not byte-for-byte replays of the complete historical prompts. No bot run, publication, production
ledger write or state mutation was used. Diagnostic cost: USD 0.03648450, all usage resolved.

- Kaiser: baseline 3/3 MATERIAL_UPDATE; variant 2/3 DUPLICATE. The remaining variant update used current-only
  altercation background, not inverted source attribution. Santino was DUPLICATE in both arms (3/3 each),
  so no causal improvement is established for that case.
- Roman: baseline 2/3 repeated inverted attribution; variant 3/3 attributed current facts correctly. It still
  retained the current article, once as NO_MATCH and twice as MATERIAL_UPDATE. The separate question of the
  promo comment's editorial independence remains under observation; this release does not force suppression.
- Dragunov and Amazing Red: both variant groups returned DUPLICATE 3/3. Baseline retained Dragunov 2/3 and
  Amazing Red 3/3 by valuing supporting context. These were not source inversions, but the same directional
  comparison improved the outcome without a new materiality threshold.
- Real RAW match-change, PAC medical findings and Crown Jewel diplomatic-warning controls stayed
  MATERIAL_UPDATE. Dragon Lee and Bloodline duplicate controls stayed DUPLICATE. The separate Dragunov
  professional-explanation control stayed eligible in both arms. These samples do not establish perfect recall.
- Anya Rune's approximately 16-hour gap is outside the approved 12-hour published history and is unaffected.

No model, provider call count per cache miss, history duration, admission preselection, card contract,
classification, soft competition, scheduling, extraction, translation or report behavior changes. The history
prompt adds tokens; any long-term net cost or quality improvement requires observation rather than assertion.
