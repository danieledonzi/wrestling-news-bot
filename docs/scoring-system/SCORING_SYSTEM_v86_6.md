# Scoring System v86.6

## True results report gate

The report bypass is limited to complete results/recap articles with:

- results/highlights/key moments/recap marker;
- recognized show anchor;
- date marker;
- no exclusion terms such as ratings, viewership, backstage, update, rumor, appearance after release.

## Future return/speculation cap

Default cap:

```text
V86_6_FUTURE_SPEC_SCORE_CAP = 72
```

Applies to articles framed as possible return dates, advertised schedule speculation, future TV hiatus returns or similar non-completed developments.

## Post-show outcomes

Concrete outcomes such as debuts, attacks, title changes, returns during a show and wins remain controlled by the post-show outcome logic and are not capped by the future speculation rule.
