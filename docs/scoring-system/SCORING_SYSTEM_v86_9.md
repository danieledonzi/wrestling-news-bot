# Scoring System v86.9

## New caps
`V86_9_COMMENTARY_CAP` defaults to `68`.

Applied to opinion/commentary titles such as:

- `reveals why`
- `doesn't think`
- `believes`
- `explains why`
- `comments on`
- `weighs in`
- pundit/commentator takes

The cap is not applied when the article contains concrete facts such as title changes, signings, injuries, arrests, media-rights deals, or official returns/debuts.

## Report priority
True-results reports are sorted ahead of normal candidates in the processing queue. Score still matters among reports and among normal news.

## Pending behavior
If a true-results report cannot be published and WordPress does not confirm it, it remains pending.
